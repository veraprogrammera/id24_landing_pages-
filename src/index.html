AI that catches mistakes

Safer, more reliable checkout for Retail & Hotel.

The Challenge in Retail

In today's retail, massive numbers of transactions are processed daily. With just a few quick taps on the screen, mistakes can occur in the checkout system. Mistakes, unusual behaviors, or deliberate manipulations can quickly become costly.

Our AI Solution

Our AI solution analyzes each transaction anonymously to detect anomalies and flag potential risks. The system detects patterns that are easily overlooked and provides store management with clear insights in real time or via reports.

The Result

  • Fewer checkout errors
  • Lower costs
  • Stronger internal control
  • Greater security for both staff and customers
